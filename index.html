Our Story

Where the Desert Meets the Table

Since 1994, Cactus has been a sanctuary for bold, honest cooking. Every dish is a love letter to Southwestern flavors — slow-braised, wood-fired, hand-pressed. We source from local farms and import only what we cannot grow ourselves.

From our legendary guacamole to our smoked brisket burritos, every plate is made with respect for the ingredient and passion for the craft.
