How It All Started

A Kitchen Born from Passion

In 1994, Chef Pablo Reyes opened Cactus with nothing but a borrowed stove, a handful of family recipes, and a dream to bring the honest flavors of the Sonoran desert to the table. What began as a twelve-seat cantina is now one of the most beloved dining destinations in the Southwest.

Over three decades, we've stayed true to what matters: sourcing locally, cooking slowly, and treating every guest like family. Our kitchen is led by the same principles Pablo wrote on a napkin the day he signed the lease.

Today Cactus seats 180 guests across our main dining room, private events space, and award-winning cactus garden patio — but the soul of that little twelve-seat kitchen still lives in every dish we serve.

"The best meals are made with the simplest intentions."
— Chef Pablo
What We Stand For

Our Values

01
Local & Seasonal
Over 85% of our ingredients come from within a 150-mile radius. We work directly with 12 farms and shift our menu with the seasons — never compromising freshness for convenience.
02
Handcrafted Always
From our tortillas pressed each morning to our salsas ground in stone molcajetes — nothing at Cactus comes from a packet, a tin or a shortcut. Everything is made by hand, every day.
03
Community First
We donate 2% of annual revenue to local food access programs, run apprenticeships for culinary students, and have never missed a neighborhood festival in thirty years.

Our Journey

Thirty Years in the Making

1994
The Beginning
Chef Pablo opens Cactus with twelve seats, a wood-burning oven, and three family recipes. The line wraps around the block on opening night.
2001
First Expansion
We double the dining room and add the cactus garden patio — now one of the most sought-after outdoor seating spots in the city.
2008
James Beard Nominee
Chef Pablo receives his first James Beard nomination for Best Chef: Southwest. The kitchen celebrates with a staff-only feast of ten courses.
2015
The Bar Program
Diego joins and launches the craft cocktail program. Within a year, the bar is independently reviewed as one of the best mezcal programs in the country.
2024
Thirty Years
We celebrated thirty years with a community dinner for 300 guests — farmers, suppliers, staff, regulars. The original twelve-seat menu was served one last time.
